Two light waves meet. Where crest meets crest it is bright. Where crest meets trough it is dark. This is interference. Daily example: colours on a soap bubble and on an oil film on wet road.

**Steady interference needs coherent waves.** Coherent means same frequency and a constant phase difference. One source is split into two. Two separate lamps cannot give a steady pattern.

This page is a hub. Full theory and solved PYQs live in the five subpages below. Total 27 PYQs (2019–2024).

### 2. Newton rings → [Interference — Newton Rings](/interference-newton)

Characteristics list (×2: 2022, 2024). Formation with air film and the Stokes $\lambda/2$ step, geometry $t = r^2/2R$, derivation $D_n^2 = 4n\lambda R$ for dark rings (×3: 2019, 2022, 2023). Refractive-index measurement with liquid film.

### 3. Fresnel biprism → [Interference — Biprism](/interference-biprism)

Virtual-source separation from prism data $d = 2a(n-1)A$. Four numerics: $\beta$ from $d$, $D$, $\lambda$ (2022); $d$ from $\beta$, $D$, $\lambda$ (2019; ×2: 2022, 2023; 2024).

### 4. Lloyd's mirror → [Interference — Lloyd's Mirror](/interference-lloyd)

Single slit + its mirror image as the coherent pair, Stokes $\lambda/2$ flip on reflection, dark centre, half field. Lloyd's vs biprism table (2022). Three fringe-width numerics (2022, 2023, 2024 — all $\beta \approx 0.59$ mm).

### 5. Thin films, Stokes, Haidinger & Fizeau → [Interference — Thin Films](/interference-thinfilm)

Parallel-film conditions in reflected and transmitted light, Stokes relations $r = -r'$, $tt' + r^2 = 1$, wedge films ($\beta = \lambda/2\mu\alpha$), Haidinger (equal inclination) and Fizeau (equal thickness) fringes. Soap-film colour numeric (2019), thin-film theory (×2: 2022, 2023; 2024).
