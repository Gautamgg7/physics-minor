## How every PYQ reads on this site

> **Q1** &nbsp; <span class="yr">2018</span><span class="yr">2019</span><span class="yr">2023</span><span class="rep">×3</span>
> Write down the relation between **D**, **P** and **E** in a dielectric medium, and show that D = ε₀E + P.
>
> **Recall.** Polarisation P is dipole moment per unit volume. Displacement D is the free-charge field. ...
>
> **Step 1.** Take a dielectric slab of area A, thickness d between parallel plates with free charge +Q. ...
> **Step 2.** Bound charges ±σ_p appear on the faces, where σ_p = P. ...
> **Step 3.** The net field inside is E = (σ_free − σ_p)/ε₀ = (D − P)/ε₀. ...
>
> **Answer.** $\boxed{\vec{D} = \varepsilon_0\vec{E} + \vec{P}}$.
>
> *Exam tip:* write "free charge vs bound charge" line first — that is where 2 of the 5 marks sit.

Each page follows that pattern. Read theory → skim an example → re-read the steps in your own words → redo without looking.
