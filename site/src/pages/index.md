## How to use these notes

1. **Pick a topic from the side rail** — every chapter is split into sub-topic pages so you read only what you need.
2. **Each PYQ card is the same:** *Recall* (the law you need), *Steps* (one line per step, no jumps), *Answer* (boxed with units), *Exam tip* (how to score the marks).
3. **The figures are captioned to their equations.** If a picture and a formula disagree on a page, that page is wrong — tell me.
4. **The math is KaTeX.** All formulas render — if you ever see raw `$..$`, refresh once and the auto-render will catch up.
